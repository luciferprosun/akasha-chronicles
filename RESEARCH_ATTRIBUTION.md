# Research attribution and evidence boundary

## Repository identity

`luciferprosun/akasha-chronicles` is a public presentation and download archive
for the LSC research line and its MHLM/MDLH process study. LuciferSun is the
repository steward recorded by `CITATION.cff`. Every archived PDF, ZIP, source
package, image, and model output retains its own embedded creator and source
metadata.

## Evidence statement

The complete repository distribution is bound by
`evidence-chain/chain.jsonl`. The manifest helps show which exact bytes were
published together and when later changes occurred. Hashing an archived item
does not claim ownership of third-party content and is not scientific
validation.

No repository-wide license is introduced by this evidence update. Existing
rights, notices, and source-specific licenses continue to apply.
