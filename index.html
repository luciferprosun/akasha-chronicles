LSC 6.2.0 // ArXiv-ready Draft

Detector-frame tensor anisotropy is the newest LSC model layer.

LSC 6.2.0 extends the public LSC 6.0 framework with an explicit rank-2 detector-frame tensor. It separates propagation from reconstructed detector response and defines observable tests: angular anisotropy, detector dependence, energy residuals and sidereal modulation.

The draft is conservative: it does not claim confirmed new physics. It is a phenomenological ansatz requiring global fits and independent constraints from BEST/GALLEX/SAGE, KATRIN, IceCube and standard three-flavor oscillation data.
